# v1.3.0

## Highlights

The sky compass is now more informative at a glance. A new window-normal legend entry marks the direction your cover faces directly, and hovering over any compass element now shows a tooltip with its name and bearing — no more guessing which wedge is which.

## Added

- **Window-normal legend entry** — the compass legend now includes a marker for the cover's perpendicular (window-normal) direction alongside sunrise, sunset, and sun position.
- **Hover tooltips on compass elements** — mousing over sun arc segments, FOV wedge, and bearing markers shows a tooltip with the element label and its azimuth value.

## Compatibility

- Home Assistant 2024.1+
- Adaptive Cover Pro integration v1.1.0+
