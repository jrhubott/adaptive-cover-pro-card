# v1.7.0

## Highlights

The Sky Compass FOV wedge now reflects the elevation band configured in the integration. When `min_elevation` or `max_elevation` are set, the orange wedge clips to an annular sector instead of a full pie slice — so the compass shows exactly where the sun is considered valid, not just the horizontal sweep.

## Added

- **Elevation-limited FOV visualization.** The orange FOV wedge now clips to the elevation band defined by `min_elevation` (near horizon, clips outer radius) and `max_elevation` (near zenith, clips inner radius). The cover-fill overlay clips to the same band for visual consistency. When neither limit is configured, the wedge renders identically to before. Inverted limits fall back to the full pie safely. The FOV tooltip also gains an elevation band suffix (e.g. `· elev 10°–60°`) when limits are present.

## Compatibility

- Home Assistant 2024.1+
- Adaptive Cover Pro integration 2.18.0+
